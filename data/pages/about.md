**Hello friend, welcome to Crafting Yarn!** I'm Freda, and I love crocheting and collecting yarn.

Pull up a chair, grab a warm cup of tea, and let me share a little story.

Once upon a time, I picked up a skein of yarn and a crochet hook with the simple goal of making my husband a scarf. I didn't know then that a few loops and knots would lead me into a whole new world of creativity and comfort. That first scarf sparked a deep passion for creating cozy, wearable pieces that bring both beauty and comfort into everyday life.

Now, Crafting Yarn is my small corner of the world where I share these handmade treasures with you. Every stitch tells a story, and every piece carries the hope that it will bring comfort, joy, and a sense of home to whoever holds it.

Thank you, truly, for being here and for letting a bit of my story become part of yours.
