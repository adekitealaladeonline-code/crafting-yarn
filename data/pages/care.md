Handmade crochet is sturdier than it looks — with a little gentle care, your piece can always stay fresh.

## Cleaning

Hand wash in cold water is recommended. Gently clean and rinse thoroughly.

Do not soak, wring, scrub harshly or twist as it may affect the shape and texture or your unique product.  

If you have to machine wash, use delicate mode, mild detergent, low temp and place in a laundry bag. 

Pat dry with a towel to remove excess water, reshape and dry flat away from direct heat or sunlight. 

## Storage

Store your pieces somewhere dry and away from direct sun.

Apply a universal waterproof spray to help keep your bag fresh.
